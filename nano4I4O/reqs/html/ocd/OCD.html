    OCD-001
    1. Smoke bench in place of a large HIL rig
    UID: OCD-001
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • OCD-002 Second Nano stands in for the device
    • OCD-003 Large benches remain for larger tasks
    • SSS-003 nanoHIL reports observations to the host
    • SSS-004 Host orchestrates firmware
    STATEMENT:

    The nanoHIL SHALL provide an inexpensive smoke test that stands in for a multi-thousand-dollar hardware-in-the-loop bench.
    RATIONALE:

    dSPACE and similar benches stay available for larger tasks. nanoHIL exists so pin-level checkout does not consume that equipment.
    COMMENT:

    Acceptance: HIL/README.md states this purpose and lists the Nano IO a smoke bench can use.
    OCD-002
    2. Second Nano stands in for the device
    UID: OCD-002
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • OCD-001 Smoke bench in place of a large HIL rig
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • SSS-001 Two classic Nano boards
    • SSS-002 Four digital checkout wires
    STATEMENT:

    nano4I4O SHALL check out four digital wires using a second Arduino Nano as the hardware under test. The same nanoHIL pins SHALL remain the attachment point when real hardware replaces that Nano.
    RATIONALE:

    The second Nano is a stand-in so the wires can be proven before a product is attached.
    COMMENT:

    Acceptance: The bench wiring uses the nanoHIL checkout pins, and the operational text allows the HUT Nano to be replaced by the real device.
    OCD-003
    3. Large benches remain for larger tasks
    UID: OCD-003
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • OCD-001 Smoke bench in place of a large HIL rig
    STATEMENT:

    The project SHALL leave larger hardware-in-the-loop work to benches such as dSPACE.
    RATIONALE:

    nanoHIL is a smoke bench. It does not replace a full real-time HIL system.
    COMMENT:

    Acceptance: OCD and HIL/README.md both limit nanoHIL to smoke tests.
