    RELATIONS (Child):

    • IRS-004 Settle time after a drive change
    STATEMENT:

    nanoHIL pins D2, D3, D4, and D5 SHALL be wired one-to-one to the same pins on the hardware under test, and the two boards SHALL share ground.

     COMMENT:

    Acceptance: The interface specification names those four pins and ground, and no other conductor is required for the checkout.
    • IRS-001
      1. Checkout pin levels UID: IRS-001 RELATIONS (Parent):
      • SSS-002 Four digital checkout wires
      STATEMENT:

      The checkout interface SHALL use digital pins D2, D3, D4, and D5. Each pin SHALL carry a logic level of 0 or 1. The boards SHALL share ground.

       COMMENT:

      Acceptance: Reader frames name only D2, D3, D4, and D5, and each level is 0 or 1.
    • IRS-004
      4. Settle time after a drive change UID: IRS-004 RELATIONS (Parent):
      • SSS-002 Four digital checkout wires
      STATEMENT:

      After changing the checkout pins, the driving image SHALL wait 10 ms before treating the new levels as settled, and SHALL hold those levels for 50 ms so the reader can sample them.

       COMMENT:

      Acceptance: The drive task delays 10 ms as the settle interval inside a 50 ms hold.

    SSS-003
    3. nanoHIL reports observations to the host UID: SSS-003 RELATIONS (Parent):
    • OCD-001 Smoke bench in place of a large HIL rig
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • SRS-004 Fail when the bench is not usable
    • SRS-005 Host parses reader text
    • IRS-002 Host serial port
    • IRS-003 Reader frame
    STATEMENT:

    The nanoHIL SHALL observe the attached device on the checkout wires. The host SHALL be the pass or fail authority for those observations.

     COMMENT:

    Acceptance: A host test fails when the reported levels do not match the expected walk, and passes when they do.
    • SRS-004
      4. Fail when the bench is not usable UID: SRS-004 RELATIONS (Parent):
      • SSS-003 nanoHIL reports observations to the host
      RELATIONS (Child):
      • STP-002 Bench absence fails the checkout
      • STD-003 Missing ports fail the checkout
      STATEMENT:

      A hardware test SHALL fail when either port is missing, when a port cannot be opened, when flashing fails, or when the bench does not produce the expected reader text. The test SHALL NOT be skipped for those conditions.

       COMMENT:

      Acceptance: With neither the CLI options nor the environment variables set, the checkout test fails with a missing-port error.
    • SRS-005
      5. Host parses reader text UID: SRS-005 RELATIONS (Parent):
      • SSS-003 nanoHIL reports observations to the host
      RELATIONS (Child):
      • STD-004 Parser frame cases
      STATEMENT:

      The host SHALL parse reader frames and compare the sampled pin levels with the expected walk. The host SHALL be the pass or fail authority.

       COMMENT:

      Acceptance: The parser accepts a complete frame and rejects a level other than 0 or 1. The walk matcher requires the expected patterns in order.
    • IRS-002
      2. Host serial port UID: IRS-002 RELATIONS (Parent):
      • SSS-003 nanoHIL reports observations to the host
      STATEMENT:

      Reader text SHALL travel on the board USB serial port at 115200 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, and 1 stop bit. Pins D0 and D1 SHALL NOT be used as checkout wires.

       COMMENT:

      Acceptance: Firmware opens the USB serial port at 115200, and the pin catalog marks D0 and D1 as the host UART.
    • IRS-003
      3. Reader frame UID: IRS-003 RELATIONS (Parent):
      • SSS-003 nanoHIL reports observations to the host
      RELATIONS (Child):
      • SDD-004 Checkout walk
      STATEMENT:

      The reading image SHALL emit one text frame per observation. The frame SHALL be the line READY, then one line SAMPLE <pin> <level> for D2, D3, D4, and D5 in that order, then the line DONE. The level SHALL be 0 or 1. The driving image SHALL stay silent on USB serial.

       COMMENT:

      Acceptance: A parser round-trip of that frame recovers the four levels, and the drive image does not write serial text.

    • SRS-003
      3. Resolve serial ports from the CLI or the environment UID: SRS-003 RELATIONS (Parent):
      • SSS-004 Host orchestrates firmware
      STATEMENT:

      The host SHALL take the nanoHIL serial port from --hil-port when that option is present, and otherwise from NANO_HIL_PORT. The host SHALL take the hardware-under-test serial port from --hut-port when that option is present, and otherwise from NANO_HUT_PORT.

       COMMENT:

      Acceptance: A unit test resolves a CLI value ahead of the environment variable, and resolves the environment variable when the CLI value is absent.
