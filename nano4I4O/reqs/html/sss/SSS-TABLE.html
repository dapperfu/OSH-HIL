        SSS-001
        Two classic Nano boards

        The bench SHALL use two Arduino Nano boards with the ATmega328P, PlatformIO board nanoatmega328, one as nanoHIL and one as the hardware under test.

        Acceptance: Both PlatformIO projects set board to nanoatmega328.

        SSS-002
        Four digital checkout wires

        nanoHIL pins D2, D3, D4, and D5 SHALL be wired one-to-one to the same pins on the hardware under test, and the two boards SHALL share ground.

        Acceptance: The interface specification names those four pins and ground, and no other conductor is required for the checkout.

        SSS-003
        nanoHIL reports observations to the host

        The nanoHIL SHALL observe the attached device on the checkout wires. The host SHALL be the pass or fail authority for those observations.

        Acceptance: A host test fails when the reported levels do not match the expected walk, and passes when they do.

        SSS-004
        Host orchestrates firmware

        The host SHALL flash the firmware image selected for the nanoHIL and the firmware image selected for the hardware under test before judging a checkout.

        Acceptance: A hardware test flashes one PlatformIO environment on each board before reading serial text.
